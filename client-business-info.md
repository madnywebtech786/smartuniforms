## 6. Content Gaps in the Current Site (opportunities for the redesign)

- No photography of actual products/uniforms/work is described anywhere — gallery is empty.
- No testimonials or client/customer proof.
- No partner/brand logos or client logos (e.g. notable businesses they supply).
- No team or "meet the founder" content, despite the story leaning on decades of experience.
- No social media presence linked.
- No pricing, quote request flow, or catalog/lookbook.
- No business hours listed.
- No clear ordering process (how a business actually becomes a customer — consultation → design → sample → bulk order, etc.) — **needs confirming with client.**
- Founding story is inconsistent (see Section 3) — needs a single accurate narrative.
- No mention of turnaround times, minimum order quantities, service area (Calgary-only vs. Alberta-wide vs. all of Canada), or shipping.

## 7. Suggested New-Site Sections (for discussion with client)

Given the client wants "a different website with more sections related to their business," candidates to propose:

1. Hero / brand statement
2. Industries we serve (expand the existing category list into a real, visual section)
3. Services (manufacturing, embroidery, sublimation, branding/logo application) explained individually
4. How it works / process (inquiry → design → sample → production → delivery)
5. Gallery / portfolio of completed uniforms (needs real photography from client)
6. About / our story (once founding story is confirmed)
7. Why choose us / differentiators
8. Testimonials / client logos (needs client to supply)
9. FAQ (MOQs, turnaround time, service area, custom design process)
10. Contact / request a quote
